The architect of our main product (who also happens to be my team lead) talks about how we implemented 1C in Dubai. He doesn’t get bogged down in technical details, so you only need a rough idea of the subject to follow along.

The video was recorded at a developer conference in Krasnodar last June, so a few things have changed since then. For example, we now have working prototypes of the application in Arabic, and our automated testing has grown so much that Miss [Vanessa](https://github.com/Pr-Mex/vanessa-automation-single) pretty much deserves her own spot on the team :-)
