The 1C:Enterprise configuration we implemented at bus terminals in Kuzbass a couple of years ago now has a nice new [website](http://avibus.pro/).

I think it’s great! Partly because it’s one of those projects chipping away at a tired old myth about 1C that just won’t die, even among IT people: that it’s dull, sluggish software for accountants, while real programmers only write C++ (or Java, JavaScript, insert your choice here), and similar nonsense.
