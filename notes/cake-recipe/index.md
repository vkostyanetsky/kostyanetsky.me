We were covering kitchens in English class: utensils, furniture, food, that sort of thing. I read out the [cake recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fXzr5iwGFfE) from Portal as an example of a dish with lots of ingredients. It went down rather well :-)

By the way, this is one of those rare cases where the localization really nails it: in the Russian version, this monologue, along with all of GLaDOS’s other lines, sounds much more expressive.
