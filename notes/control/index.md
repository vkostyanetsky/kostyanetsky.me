I have two complaints about this game. First, its Altered Items: their descriptions feel a bit tame next to the [SCP Foundation](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCP_Foundation) writers’ wild ideas. There are some pleasant exceptions, but this part still feels weaker than the rest of the game’s paranormal material.

My second gripe is where they chose to play [My Dark Disquet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CkojH7htq-k) by Poets of the Fall. Damn it, that track is far too good for such an unremarkable section! I expected it during the credits or some crazy fight.

I know both complaints sound a bit silly, but that really is all I’ve got. Control looks fantastic, plays brilliantly, and tells a great story. The only reason I won’t be giving anyone this game for New Year is that the Epic Games Store doesn’t support gifting yet :-)
