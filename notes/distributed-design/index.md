A designer at Stack Overflow [wrote](http://www.tedgoas.com/blog/distributed-design) about how they organize remote work. I liked the structure: it doesn’t just say “here’s how we do it,” but pairs each problem with a solution.

Almost all development at our company is remote, and I work remotely too. I really like working this way; the main thing is remembering how easy it is to pick up unexpected problems when your workplace and your home are the same place. Take the advice to drink water and get some sun: during a crunch, those things really are easy to forget.
