A fairly short story, but it makes an impression. You can’t lose, and there isn’t much choice beyond dialogue options. But the sound, the visuals, and the beautifully written characters are surprising and touching, even when you guess the plot twists before they happen.

Overall, the game reminded me of something between Firewatch and The Vanishing of Ethan Carter. I’d have liked a slightly longer story and a lot more drama, but I still loved how it ended.
