Kat’s handwriting makes experienced doctors choke with envy. Today, if her note is to be believed, she asked me to pick up four screw buckets and twelve telepathic eagles on my way home.

Never mind the screws, but where am I supposed to find eagles? Telepathic ones, at that?

I’m lost. My life has been wasted.
