https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsFQmXdC29g

Rock! Ro-o-ock! :-)

Seriously, I’ve [written](/notes/i-wish-i-could/) about this before, but I’m happy to repeat myself: things like this are one of the simplest and most enjoyable ways to memorize lots of words and expressions.

Sure, there are plenty of sharp-minded people who don’t need this trick. It’s more for ordinary folks like me. Otherwise, all those “what a good match you and I” and “spellbound by your beauty pure and true” phrases sometimes evaporate from our heads in, well… Ten seconds?

This way, you watch the video, catch yourself singing along, and the words stick before you know it.
