A short, vivid adventure game. No challenge, just a family story where the parents got so caught up in their own problems that they missed something really important.

I think the sister’s journals could have been removed entirely: the story comes through perfectly in the objects, letters, notes, and photographs. It would have left more mystery, more room to figure things out for yourself.

Though I have to admit, as it stands, it’s damn touching.
