Excruciatingly tedious gameplay that boils down to finding scraps of paper. A limp, disjointed plot cobbled together from mystical theories about the Dyatlov group’s death. As horror, it’s a complete zero. There’s little to fear: just blind monsters and occasional jump scares.

The only good bits are the visuals and sound. The people behind those did a fantastic job. Sadly, what you end up with is just a huge map with almost nobody in it, almost nothing in it, almost nothing to do, and, worst of all, almost no reason to do anything.
