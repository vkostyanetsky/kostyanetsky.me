A great adaptation of [Papers, Please](https://store.steampowered.com/app/239030/Papers_Please/), despite being only ten minutes long. Savochkin is excellent, and everyone else delivers too, including the screenwriter. Fitting both drama and a plot twist into that running time takes skill.

Considering that video game adaptations rarely set the world on fire, this one’s a real gem.
