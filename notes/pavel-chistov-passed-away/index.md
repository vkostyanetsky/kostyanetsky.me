Pavel Chistov died of heart failure on Friday evening. If you don’t know who he was, here is an [obituary](https://infostart.ru/journal/news/news/ushel-iz-zhizni-pavel-chistov_1490373/). Though if you read this blog, you probably do.

[Nikita Gryzlov](https://t.me/nixel2007_thoughts/205), [Ilya Leontyev](https://t.me/ilyaleontyevpro1c/38946), and [Sergey Gorshenin](https://t.me/cio2cdto/514) are raising money to support Pavel’s family. His widow’s card number is also listed at the end of the obituary linked above.

Please take care of yourselves.
