An incredibly addictive farming simulator made with enormous attention to detail. People who know what they’re talking about will tell you about the soundtrack, the lovely pixel art, and all that. I’m no good at that stuff. I just bloody loved it, that’s all :-)

Actually, I will say something about the soundtrack. During winter, the game sometimes plays [Ancient](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zr-PHm_qLgg), one of the simplest yet most atmospheric melodies I’ve ever heard in a video game.
