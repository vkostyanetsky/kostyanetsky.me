A lovely isometric exploration game about a town filled with classic Japanese ghosts and a girl looking for her missing sister. It’s not all that scary; mostly it keeps you on edge. Not all that hard either, though you do have to pay attention: there are lots of ghosts, they all behave differently, and you can’t afford to wander around daydreaming. It’s not all that fast, though you’ll need quick reflexes here and there, especially near the end.

I came away feeling really good about it. Maybe they didn’t have millions to spend, but you can tell they tried to make the best game they could.
